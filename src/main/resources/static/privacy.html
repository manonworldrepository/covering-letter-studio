Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Introduction

Cover Letter Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal tool for generating tailored cover letters and motivation messages using Google Gemini AI. This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use our application.

Data We Collect

When you use Cover Letter Studio, the following data may be stored or processed:

  • Google account information — Your email address and display name are collected during Google Sign-In to authenticate your identity and restrict access to authorized users.
  • Resume and document content — Uploaded resumes and supporting documents are stored locally in your application session for use in cover letter generation.
  • Job descriptions — Text you enter as job descriptions is used to generate cover letters and is sent to Google Gemini for processing.
  • Generated content — Cover letters and motivation messages generated by the app are stored in your application session.
  • Application settings — Your Gemini API key, selected model, and preferences are stored in your application session.

How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication — Your Google account email is used to verify you are an authorized user of the app.
  • Cover letter generation — Your resume content, supporting documents, and job descriptions are sent to Google Gemini AI to generate tailored cover letters.
  • Data persistence — Your data (resumes, documents, settings, applied jobs) is stored in a Google Cloud Storage bucket to persist across application restarts.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — Used for authentication. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Gemini AI — Used for generating cover letters. Your resume text, job descriptions, and supporting document content are sent to Google's Gemini API. Google's AI principles and data handling policies apply: ai.google/responsibility/
  • Google Cloud Run — The application is hosted on Google Cloud Run. Google's cloud privacy notice applies: cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice
  • Google Cloud Storage — Used to store your application data (resumes, documents, settings). Data is stored in a private bucket accessible only by the application.

Data Storage and Retention

Your application data is stored in Google Cloud Storage and persists across sessions. The data is kept in a private bucket that is not accessible to the public. You can delete your data by clearing your application session or contacting us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as necessary for the app to function.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • The application is accessible only via HTTPS (encrypted connections)
  • Access is restricted to authorized Google accounts
  • Cloud Storage data is stored in private buckets
  • Your Gemini API key is stored as a secure environment variable on Cloud Run

Your Rights

As the sole user of this personal application, you have full control over your data. You can:

  • Access and view all your stored data through the application
  • Delete individual resumes, documents, or applied jobs
  • Clear all application data by removing items through the app interface

Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at info@manonworld.de.
